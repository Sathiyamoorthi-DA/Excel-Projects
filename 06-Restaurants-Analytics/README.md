## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Restaurant Analytics Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Excel to monitor restaurant sales performance, profitability, customer activity, menu item performance, payment methods, dining preferences, and service staff performance.

The dashboard transforms restaurant transaction data into meaningful business insights through KPI cards, interactive slicers, monthly sales and profit trends, city-wise performance, category-wise sales analysis, and customer segmentation visualizations.

It provides management with a consolidated view of restaurant operations to evaluate business performance, identify popular menu items, monitor customer preferences, analyze profitability, and support better operational and business decisions.

### 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total sales and order volume.
- Analyze overall restaurant profitability.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends.
- Compare sales performance across different cities.
- Identify top-selling menu items by quantity.
- Evaluate sales contribution by food category.
- Monitor waiter-wise sales performance.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Understand payment method distribution.
- Evaluate dining type preferences.
- Monitor average customer ratings by food category.
- Support restaurant sales and operational planning.
