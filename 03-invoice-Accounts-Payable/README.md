## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Business Performance Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Excel to monitor end-to-end invoice processing, payment performance, processing cycle times, regional business activity, and financial operations.

The dashboard transforms invoice and payment data into meaningful business insights through KPI cards, interactive filters, monthly trends, payment analysis, and operational performance visualizations.

It provides management with a consolidated view of invoice processing activities, payment methods, outstanding payable days, and exception rates to support efficient financial operations and data-driven decision-making.

### 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total invoice volume and invoice amounts.
- Track end-to-end invoice processing cycle time.
- Analyze invoice creation and posting timelines.
- Monitor invoice exceptions and processing efficiency.
- Compare invoice processing performance across users.
- Analyze regional invoice distribution.
- Track monthly payment spending trends.
- Monitor electronic versus manual payments.
- Evaluate Days Payable Outstanding (DPO).
- Identify invoice status and payment processing patterns.

**Key analysis:**
- Received-to-Created processing time.
- Created-to-Posted processing time.
- Posted-to-Paid processing time.
- Comparison of processing durations.
- Identification of processing delays.

Evaluates invoice processing performance across business regions.

**Regions Covered:**
- East
