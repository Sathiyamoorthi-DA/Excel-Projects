## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Insurance Sales & Claims Settlement Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Excel to monitor insurance sales performance, policy distribution, premium revenue, claim amounts, claim settlements, and customer activity.

The dashboard transforms insurance data into meaningful business insights through KPI cards, interactive slicers, monthly revenue trends, insurance-type comparisons, regional analysis, and sales channel performance visualizations.

It provides management with a consolidated view of insurance operations to evaluate business performance, monitor policy renewals, analyze claims settlement efficiency, and support better sales and operational decisions.

### 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total customers and policies issued.
- Analyze total premium revenue and average premium.
- Evaluate claim settlement amounts and settlement rates.
- Monitor policy renewal performance.
- Analyze monthly premium revenue trends.
- Compare premium revenue across insurance types.
- Evaluate claim amounts against settlement amounts.
- Analyze sales performance across different channels.
- Monitor premium revenue across geographical regions.
- Track active, expired, and lapsed policies.
- Understand claim status distribution.
- Support insurance sales and claims management decisions.

## 📊 Dashboard Features & Analysis

### 1️⃣ Insurance Business Overview

Provides a consolidated overview of insurance business performance through key operational and financial indicators.

**Key analysis:**
- Total customer count.
- Total policies issued.
- Overall premium revenue.
- Total claim settlement amount.
- Claim settlement rate.
- Average premium value.
- Policy renewal rate.

### 2️⃣ Monthly Premium Revenue Trend

Evaluates monthly premium revenue performance across January to December.

**Key analysis:**
- Monthly premium revenue.
- Month-wise revenue comparison.
- Identification of high-revenue months.
- Monthly sales performance trends.
- Revenue fluctuations throughout the year.

**Business Insight:**

May recorded the highest displayed monthly premium revenue at approximately ₹5,979K, while April recorded approximately ₹4,305K.

### 3️⃣ Premium Revenue by Insurance Type

Analyzes premium revenue distribution across three major insurance categories.

**Insurance Types Covered:**
- Health Insurance
- Life Insurance
- Term Insurance

**Key analysis:**
- Insurance-type-wise premium revenue.
- Revenue contribution by insurance category.
- Comparison of insurance product performance.
- Identification of major revenue-generating categories.

**Business Insight:**

Life Insurance recorded the highest displayed premium revenue at ₹32,142K, followed by Health Insurance at ₹18,054K and Term Insurance at ₹8,305K.

### 4️⃣ Claim Amount vs Settlement Amount

Compares total claim amounts against actual claim settlement amounts across insurance categories.

**Insurance Types Covered:**
- Health
- Life
- Term

**Key analysis:**
- Total claim amount by insurance type.
- Claim settlement amount comparison.
- Insurance-category-wise claim analysis.
- Identification of differences between claimed and settled amounts.
- Monitoring claims processing performance.

### 5️⃣ Sales Channel Performance

Evaluates premium revenue generated through different insurance sales channels.

**Sales Channels Covered:**
- Partner
- Online
- Branch
- Agent

**Key analysis:**
- Channel-wise premium revenue.
- Sales channel contribution.
- Revenue comparison across channels.
- Identification of high-performing sales channels.
- Channel performance monitoring.

**Business Insight:**

Branch recorded the highest displayed premium revenue at ₹15,162K, followed by Agent at ₹14,688K, Partner at ₹14,414K, and Online at ₹14,237K.

### 6️⃣ Premium Revenue by Region

Analyzes geographical premium revenue distribution across four regions.

**Regions Covered:**
- South
- West
- North
- East

**Key analysis:**
- Region-wise premium revenue.
- Geographical sales comparison.
- Regional revenue contribution.
- Identification of high-performing regions.
- Regional business performance monitoring.

**Business Insight:**

South recorded the highest displayed premium revenue at ₹36,262K, indicating its significant contribution to overall insurance revenue.

### 7️⃣ Policy Status by Insurance Type

Monitors policy distribution across different insurance categories based on policy status.

**Policy Status Categories:**
- Active
- Expired
- Lapsed

**Key analysis:**
- Active policy monitoring.
- Expired policy tracking.
- Lapsed policy identification.
- Insurance-type-wise policy comparison.
- Policy retention and renewal monitoring.

### 8️⃣ Claim Status Breakdown

Provides a visual breakdown of insurance claims based on their current status.

**Claim Status Categories:**
- No Claim
- Rejected
- Settled
- Under Review

**Key analysis:**
- Claim status distribution.
- Percentage contribution by claim status.
- Settled claim monitoring.
- Rejected claim analysis.
- Pending claim identification.

**Business Insight:**

The displayed claim status distribution shows approximately 89% No Claim, 8% Rejected, 1% Settled, and 2% Under Review.

### 9️⃣ Total Policy Sales Trend

Tracks monthly policy sales performance from January to December.

**Key analysis:**
- Monthly policy sales volume.
- Month-wise policy comparison.
- Identification of high-performing months.
- Policy sales fluctuations.
- Monthly sales performance monitoring.

**Business Insight:**

May recorded the highest displayed policy sales volume at 182 policies, while April recorded the lowest at 128 policies.

| Claim Status | No Claim, Rejected, Settled, Under Review |

These interactive filters help users analyze insurance performance based on reporting year, month, customer gender, claim status, and policy status.
