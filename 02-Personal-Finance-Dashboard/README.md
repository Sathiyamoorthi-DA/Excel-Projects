## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Personal Finance Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Excel to monitor income, expenses, savings, budget utilization, account balances, and financial transactions.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of personal financial activities through KPI cards, monthly financial trends, category-wise analysis, budget comparisons, and interactive slicers.

It helps users understand their spending patterns, monitor savings goals, track account balances, and make better financial decisions through data-driven insights.

### 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total income and expenses.
- Calculate net savings and savings rate.
- Track monthly income and expense trends.
- Analyze expenses across different spending categories.
- Monitor savings goals and budget utilization.
- Compare actual spending against planned budgets.
- Track transactions across different banks.
- Analyze income sources and payment methods.

## 📉 Visualizations Used

The dashboard combines multiple Excel chart types to present financial information.

- KPI Cards – Income, expenses, savings, and budget indicators.
- Line Chart – Monthly income and expense trends.
- Doughnut Chart – Expense category distribution.
- Column Chart – Savings goals by amount.
- Waterfall Chart – Transactions by bank.
- Column Chart – Income by category.
- Pie Chart – Account-wise balance distribution.
- Clustered Column Chart – Budget versus actual expenses.
- Horizontal Bar Chart – Payment method-wise expenses.
- Interactive Slicers – Month and account selection.
