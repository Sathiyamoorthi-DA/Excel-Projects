## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Excel to monitor online sales performance, profitability, customer activity, product sales, payment methods, order fulfillment, and shipping performance.

The dashboard transforms e-commerce transaction data into meaningful business insights through KPI cards, interactive slicers, monthly sales trends, category-wise revenue analysis, state-wise sales performance, top product rankings, and customer segmentation visualizations.

It provides management with a consolidated view of e-commerce operations to evaluate business performance, identify high-performing products, understand customer purchasing behavior, monitor order statuses, and support better sales and operational decisions.

### 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total sales revenue and order volume.
- Analyze overall business profitability.
- Track monthly sales performance.
- Compare sales across different product categories.
- Evaluate geographical sales performance by state.
- Identify top 10 products by sales value.
- Analyze top 10 customers by sales contribution.
- Monitor payment method distribution.
- Track cancelled, delivered, pending, and returned orders.
- Analyze shipping mode preferences.
- Understand customer gender distribution.
- Support e-commerce sales and operational planning.

## 📉 Visualizations Used

The dashboard combines multiple Excel chart types to present e-commerce business insights.

- KPI Cards – Overall e-commerce business performance.
- Combo Chart – Monthly sales trend using columns and a line.
- Doughnut Chart – Sales by product category.
- Line Chart – State-wise sales performance.
- Horizontal Bar / Data Bar Analysis – Top 10 products by sales.
- Ranked Table – Top 10 customers by sales.
- Doughnut Chart – Payment mode by orders.
- Doughnut Chart – Order status distribution.
- Doughnut Chart – Shipping mode distribution.
- Interactive Slicers – Month and Gender.
