## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Employee Performance Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Excel to monitor employee productivity, achievement, task completion, quality scores, and departmental performance.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of workforce performance through KPI cards, interactive slicers, charts, and monthly performance trends.

It helps analyze employee contributions, compare department performance, monitor task completion, and identify areas for workforce improvement.

### 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall employee performance and productivity.
- Analyze achievement levels across departments.
- Track completed and pending employee tasks.
- Evaluate monthly productivity trends.
- Compare employee performance across different regions.
- Identify top-performing employees.
- Monitor employee quality scores and performance categories.

## 📊 Dashboard Features & Analysis

### 1️⃣ Employee Performance Overview

Provides a consolidated overview of employee productivity and performance.

**Key indicators:**
- Total employee count.
- Average productivity.
- Average achievement.
- Completed tasks.
- Average quality score.

### 2️⃣ Department Performance Analysis

Evaluates achievement and productivity across different departments.

**Departments Covered:**
- Finance
- HR
- IT
- Marketing
- Operations
- Sales

**Key analysis:**
- Average achievement by department.
- Average productivity by department.
- Department-level performance comparison.
- Identification of high and low-performing departments.

### 3️⃣ Monthly Productivity Trend

Tracks employee productivity across different months.

**Key analysis:**
- Monthly average productivity.
- Productivity fluctuations.
- Month-wise performance comparison.
- Identification of productivity improvement opportunities.

### 4️⃣ Regional Performance Analysis

Compares employee achievement across different geographical regions.

**Regions Covered:**
- California
- Florida
- New York
- Texas

**Key analysis:**
- Average achievement by state.
- Regional performance comparison.
- Identification of high-performing regions.

### 5️⃣ Task Completion Analysis

Monitors completed and pending tasks across reporting months.

**Key analysis:**

## 📉 Visualizations Used

The dashboard uses multiple Excel chart types to present workforce insights.

- KPI Cards – Employee and performance indicators.
- Column Chart – Average achievement by department.
- Doughnut Chart – Average productivity by department.
- Line Chart – Monthly average productivity trend.
- Horizontal Bar Chart – Average achievement by state.
- Stacked Column Chart – Completed vs. pending tasks.
- Horizontal Bar Chart – Top 5 employees by achievement.
- Interactive Slicers – Region, department, and performance category.
