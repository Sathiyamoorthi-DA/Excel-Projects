## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Personal Finance Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Excel to monitor personal income, expenses, savings, investments, net worth, financial goals, and monthly financial performance.

The dashboard transforms personal financial transactions into meaningful financial insights through KPI cards, year-over-year comparisons, interactive slicers, income versus expense trends, expense breakdowns, investment allocation, savings analysis, and financial goal tracking.

It provides a consolidated view of personal financial performance to understand spending patterns, monitor savings progress, evaluate investment distribution, track financial targets, and support better financial planning and decision-making.

### 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total income and monthly earnings.
- Track total expenses and spending patterns.
- Analyze overall savings and savings rate.
- Monitor investment contributions and allocation.
- Calculate and track personal net worth.
- Compare income and expenses year over year.
- Identify major expense categories.
- Analyze different income sources.
- Monitor monthly savings performance.
- Track financial goals and achievement percentages.
- Evaluate investment distribution across asset categories.
- Support better budgeting and financial planning decisions.

## 📊 Dashboard Features & Analysis

### 1️⃣ Personal Financial Overview

Provides a consolidated overview of personal financial performance through key financial indicators.

**Key analysis:**
- Total income generated.
- Total expenses incurred.
- Total savings accumulated.
- Total investment value.
- Overall net worth.
- Year-over-year financial growth.

**Business Insight:**

Total income increased by 12%, while savings increased by 20% compared with the previous year. Net worth recorded an 18% increase.

### 2️⃣ Income vs Expenses Analysis

Compares monthly income and expenses to understand financial performance and cash flow throughout the year.

**Key analysis:**
- Monthly income tracking.
- Monthly expense monitoring.
- Income versus expense comparison.
- Monthly financial surplus.
- Identification of income and spending trends.
- Year-over-year performance comparison.

**Business Insight:**

The dashboard shows income remaining above expenses throughout the displayed monthly trend, indicating a positive monthly financial surplus.

### 3️⃣ Expenses Breakdown

Analyzes total expenses across eight major spending categories.

**Expense Categories Covered:**
- Housing
- Food
- EMI/Debt
- Transportation
- Insurance
- Utilities
- Shopping
- Others

**Key analysis:**
- Category-wise expense distribution.

Housing represents the largest expense category, contributing 40.5% of total expenses, followed by Food at 17.3% and EMI/Debt at 10.9%.
