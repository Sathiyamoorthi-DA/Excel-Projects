## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Inventory Analytics Dashboard**, developed using Microsoft Excel to monitor inventory valuation, stock quantities, sales and purchase performance, warehouse operations, supplier contributions, and stock availability.

The dashboard transforms inventory data into meaningful business insights through KPI cards, interactive slicers, monthly trends, category-wise analysis, and warehouse performance visualizations.

It provides management with a consolidated view of inventory operations to monitor stock levels, identify low-stock items, evaluate supplier performance, and support better inventory planning and purchasing decisions.

### 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total inventory value and stock quantity.
- Identify low-stock and out-of-stock items.
- Analyze monthly sales and purchase trends.
- Evaluate inventory value across product categories.
- Monitor products exceeding reorder levels.
- Analyze inventory turnover by category.
- Compare purchase values across suppliers.
- Track stock quantities across warehouses.
- Support inventory planning and stock control.
